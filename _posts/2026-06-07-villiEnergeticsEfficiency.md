Derivations used in our <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04831">recent paper</a> on villi-pumped efficiency and energetics.

## Stokes second problem
Problem concerns an oscillating (with circular frequency $$\omega$$) flat-plate bounding a semi-infinite fluid of dynamic viscosity $$\mu$$. The problem is described [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes_problem).

Derivation for the total viscous dissipation over flat-plate length $$P$$ [(PDF)](/assets/pdf/Stokes-Second-Problem-dissipation.pdf).

## Long-wavelength peristaltis derivation
Problem concerns flow generared by propagating peristaltic wave moving along the walls of a thin 2D channel. The solution is obtained in [(Shapiro,
Jaffrin & Weinberg 1969)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/abs/peristaltic-pumping-with-long-wavelengths-at-low-reynolds-number/792DAD9B680C5074E3E674844DDB79FE?utm_campaign=shareaholic&utm_medium=copy_link&utm_source=bookmark).

The solution is derived using the same notation as in *Shapiro et. al. (1969)* [(PDF)](/assets/pdf/shapiroPeristalsis1969_solution.pdf).

Derivation for the wall-shear and channel vorticity scales for a peristaltic wavelength of $$\lambda$$.
